If you tilt easily or play impulsively

patience above everything.


"4. Straight Sports Bets"

A moneyline or spread bet is one decision 
with transparent odds. The house edge 
comes from the vig — usually 4-5% on 
standard -110 lines.

That is far better than a parlay, which 
multiplies the vig on every leg.

Straight bets are where discipline and 
information give you the best real shot 
at a positive expected value over time.
