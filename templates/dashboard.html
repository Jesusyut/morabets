BETTING STRATEGY GUIDE

💰 1. Bankroll Management

The foundation of profitable betting is proper bankroll management:

  • Unit System: Bet 1–5% of your total bankroll per wager
  • High Confidence: 3–5% of bankroll (60%+ hit probability)
  • Medium Confidence: 2–3% of bankroll (50–59%)
  • Low/Speculative: 1–2% of bankroll (under 50%)
Example: With a $300 bankroll, your max bet should be $15 (5%) on high-confidence plays.

👥 2. Player Diversification

Avoid correlation risk by diversifying your selections:

  • Different Teams: Don't stack multiple players from the same team
  • Different Games: Spread picks across multiple matchups
  • Different Stat Types: Mix hitting, pitching, and fielding props
  • Avoid Same-Game Parlays: One bad game kills multiple legs

📋 3. Optimal Slip Sizing

Structure your bet slips for maximum profitability:

  • 3–4 Pick Maximum: Sweet spot for risk vs. reward
  • Focus on Expected Value: Target positive EV over big payouts
  • Quality Over Quantity: Fewer, well-researched bets
  • Single Bets: Consider straight bets on high-confidence props

How Mora Bets Works

Mora Bets pulls daily MLB and NHL betting lines from FanDuel, DraftKings, and BetMGM, then removes the sportsbook's built-in margin — called the vig — to calculate the true probability each line represents. This is called no-vig probability, and it tells you what the sharp market actually believes about each game before the house edge distorts the number.

Every pick on today's board is sorted by true probability — highest confidence first. The best available odds line across all major books is shown on each card so you can always bet at the best price. This is called line shopping and over a full MLB season it compounds into hundreds of dollars in additional profit from the same picks.

What Is No-Vig Probability?

Standard -110 odds imply a 52.4% chance of winning — but that includes the sportsbook's 4.5% margin. Strip the vig and both sides of a 50/50 bet show 50% each, which is the true market probability. When one side shows 60% true probability, the market is genuinely pricing that outcome as a clear favorite — not just because of the vig.
