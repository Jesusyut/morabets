The Math Behind True Probability

Every prop market has two sides — the over and the under.

Each side has an implied probability baked into the odds.

When you add those two implied probabilities together they always exceed 100%. The excess is the vig — the house's cut.

No-vig probability removes that excess and redistributes it equally, giving you the true market consensus on the outcome.

Over -150 → implied 60.0%
Under +120 → implied 45.5%
Total: 105.5% (the 5.5% is the vig)

No-vig over: 60.0 / 105.5 = 56.9%
No-vig under: 45.5 / 105.5 = 43.1%

Now you know what the market actually thinks. Not what the sportsbook wants you to think.
